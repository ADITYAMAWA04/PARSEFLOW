Turn documents into text you can check.

ParseFlow converts documents of almost any kind, whether digital, scanned or handwritten, into Markdown and structured JSON. Every paragraph, table, equation and figure keeps a link to the page region it came from, so any line can be traced back to its source in one click.

Choose a document or drop it herePDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images (JPG, PNG, TIFF...), text, HTML, Markdown, CSV, EPUB and more, of any length.

What it reads

  • Digital PDFs, including multi-column papers and long reports, with no page limit
  • Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text, HTML, Markdown, CSV, EPUB and image files (TIFF, PNG, JPG, WebP...)
  • Tables, keeping merged cells
  • Equations, written out as LaTeX
  • Scanned pages, with offline OCR
  • Handwriting, figures and screenshots, with a connected AI model

How results are marked

  • VerifiedConfirmed by an independent source, such as the PDF's own text.
  • ReviewRead by one method only. Worth a glance against the page.
  • Not readCould not be read. The region is kept for you to check.

Ask about it

Ask questions, or for a summary or practice questions. Answers cite the pages they rely on and label anything that comes from outside the document. Without an AI model, Ask quotes the closest passages instead.
